**a) FPB(56,14)**

1. Find the larger number: 56 > 14
2. Divide the larger number by the smaller one: 56 ÷ 14 = 4 with a remainder of 0
3. Since the remainder is 0, the GCD is the divisor: 14

So, FPB(56,14) = 14

**b) FPB(77,33)**

1. Find the larger number: 77 > 33
2. Divide the larger number by the smaller one: 77 ÷ 33 = 2 with a remainder of 11
3. Divide the larger number by the remainder: 33 ÷ 11 = 3 with a remainder of 0
4. Since the remainder is 0, the GCD is the divisor: 11

So, FPB(77,33) = 11
