A classic problem! Let's find the shortest path from A to E.

We can use the following steps:

1. Find the minimum weight edges coming out of A:
	* A-B (4)
	* A-C (2)
Choose the lighter edge: A-C (2)
2. Move to vertex C:
	* Add the weight of the edge A-C (2) to our total weight
3. From C, find the minimum weight edges going to E:
	* C-E (10)
	* D-E (2)
Choose the lighter edge: D-E (2)
4. Move to vertex E:
	* Add the weight of the edge D-E (2) to our total weight

Now, let's recap the path:

A -> C (weight 2)
C -> D (no need to calculate; we're just moving to the next node)
D -> E (weight 2)

The total weight of the shortest path from A to E is: 2 + 2 = 4
