Here are my identifications:

1. "Aku pusing" - Symptom
	* Akar persoalan: Stress or fatigue due to various reasons (e.g., academic pressure, personal issues, lack of sleep)
2. "Aku terlambat kuliah" - Problem
	* Akar persoalan: Poor time management, procrastination, or lack of prioritization in completing assignments and attending classes on time
3. "Banyak supermarket tutup" - Symptom
	* Akar persoalan: Limited shopping options due to factors like:
		+ Changes in the business environment (e.g., market saturation, competition)
		+ Demographic changes in the area (e.g., shifting consumer preferences, population growth)
		+ Regulatory or logistical issues affecting the supermarkets' operations

In general:

* Symptoms are indicators of a problem or issue. They are often subjective experiences that can be caused by various factors.
* Problems are underlying issues that need to be addressed. They can have multiple symptoms, and solving the problem often requires identifying and addressing its root causes.

By recognizing whether something is a symptom or a problem, we can focus on finding solutions to the underlying issue rather than just treating the symptom!
