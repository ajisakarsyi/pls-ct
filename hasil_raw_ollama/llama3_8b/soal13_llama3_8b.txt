Here is the algorithm for making instant noodles (mie instan):

**Input**

* 1 package of instant noodles
* Water or hot water
* Optional: seasonings, such as soy sauce, chili flakes, or sesame oil

**Proses**

1. Open the package of instant noodles and remove any seasoning packets.
2. Measure out the recommended amount of water according to the package instructions. For most brands, this is 2-3 cups (400-600 ml) for a single serving.
3. Bring the measured water to a boil in a pot or kettle.
4. Once boiling, pour the boiling water over the instant noodles in a separate bowl or container.
5. Let the noodles sit for 2-3 minutes, allowing them to rehydrate and cook.
6. If desired, add any additional seasonings (e.g., soy sauce, chili flakes, sesame oil) according to personal taste.
7. Stir well to distribute the seasonings evenly.

**Output**

* A steaming hot bowl of cooked instant noodles, ready to be enjoyed!

Note: This algorithm assumes a basic preparation method for making instant noodles. Some brands may have specific instructions or variations on this process, so please refer to the package instructions if you're unsure.
